GroundIQ connects emails, Telegram messages, schedules and CAD plans into a shared construction-site record. It detects conflicts such as closing walls before inspection, working from outdated revisions or starting tasks out of sequence. AI extracts information and explains problems; deterministic rules raise source-linked flags. A 3D dashboard shows affected areas, evidence and proposed actions. Unconfirmed claims cannot satisfy prerequisites until a human reviews and confirms the evidence. Approving actions and closing flags are separate human decisions. Automated warnings, morning scans and escalation keep problems visible. Feedback corrects specific extraction errors without weakening safety checks. MCP lets Talona or other agents use the same context engine. Built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js and Three.js, it includes a reproducible offline demo using synthetic data. The MVP requires production authentication and compliance work before real deployment.
